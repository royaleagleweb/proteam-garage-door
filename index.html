Hurricane Season

Protect Your Home Before the Storm

Your garage door is the biggest opening in your house. If it fails during a hurricane, wind gets in and can take the roof off. We install impact-rated doors that meet Florida code.

Rated for 180+ MPH Winds

Our doors meet Miami-Dade HVHZ and Florida Building Code requirements. We handle the permit and inspection too.

Stops Flying Debris

Reinforced steel bracing and impact-rated glass. These doors are built to take a hit from whatever the storm throws at them.

Lower Your Insurance

Florida law says your insurance company has to give you a discount for wind protection. A lot of our customers save $500+ a year.

Adds Value to Your Home

A new garage door is one of the best home upgrades for resale value. Hurricane-rated? Even better — buyers love it.
